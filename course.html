§ 01 By the end of the course

Five capabilities that will quietly reshape how you practice.

01

Acquire diagnostic-quality images

Standardized probe positioning for shoulder, iliopsoas, stifle, tarsus, and carpus. No more "I can't find the structure."

02

Recognize tendon & ligament injury

Recognize normal tendinous and ligamentous patterns — and the disruption that signals injury — at clinically useful magnification.

03

Localize the lesion

Layer-by-layer evaluation strategies that turn vague lameness into a specific, anatomically localized diagnosis.

04

Qualify for remote reads

Complete the modules, homework, and the remote-read qualification — then submit live cases from your own practice to Dr. Canapp for a second-opinion read. A feedback loop most CE never offers.

05

Talk to clients with images

Sit down with the owner of an athlete and explain what you found — with the actual scan in hand.
