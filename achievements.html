§ 03 Beyond publication

Peer review, board service,and mentorship.

Dr. Canapp serves as a peer reviewer for veterinary journals, has served on the ACVSMR specialty board's exam committee, and has mentored a generation of sports medicine residents and rehabilitation interns.

Peer review

Editorial reviewer for Clinician's Brief (2013–present), DVM 360 (2012–present), and Veterinary Surgery (2008–present).

ACVSMR specialty board service

Canine Chair of the ACVSMR Exam Committee (2013–2015) and Exam Committee member (2012–2015) — the diplomate certification body for veterinary sports medicine & rehabilitation.

Mentorship & education

Personally grades course homework and mentors trained clinicians through the remote-read pathway. Faculty advisor to numerous ACVSMR residents and VOSM sports medicine interns over the past decade.
