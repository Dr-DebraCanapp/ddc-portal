§ 02 Purpose

Helping diagnose what other imaging quietly misses, and teaching the modality to the veterinarians of tomorrow.

Ultrasound sees soft tissue in motion — tendon, muscle and ligament pathology that films and CT can only infer. The practice rests on two pillars: the clinic, and the classroom.
